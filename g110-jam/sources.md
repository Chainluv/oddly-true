## Notes and changes from the original script
- **"Imagine being stuck in traffic for 12 days"** became **"This traffic jam lasted 12 days."** The jam lasted about 12 days, but the longest any driver was reported stuck is five days.
- **The water price:** reports say 10 yuan in some places and 15 in others, so the video says "up to fifteen".
- **"They sleep in their cabs"** was cut because I couldn't verify it. Playing cards is from CNN.
- **"Villagers on bicycles"** became "locals selling food and water", because no source mentions bicycles.
- **The length:** reports range from 10 to 12 days and from about 62 miles to over 100 km. The video uses the Wikipedia figures (12 days, 100+ km).
- **The map** is stylized and labeled "STYLIZED MAP". The trucks and people are generic shapes.
