## Notes and disputes
- **Final payment year:** most sources say 1979, but [CFR](https://www.cfr.org/articles/twe-remembers-black-tom-explosion) says 1978. The video uses 1979, the majority figure.
- **Maryland:** the idea note had people in Maryland mistaking it for an earthquake, but I found only weak support for that, so it's left out. The map's pulse rings reach Pennsylvania without naming Maryland.
- **Death toll:** reports vary (Wikipedia says "at least seven", other accounts fewer), so the video gives no number.
- **Settlement details:** reported settlement amounts and dates vary (1939 award, 1950s agreement), so the video shows only 1939 (ruling) and 1979 (last payment).
- **Maps** are stylized reconstructions and labeled "STYLIZED MAP". The statue is a generic silhouette.
